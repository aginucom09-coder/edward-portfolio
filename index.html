About

A bit about me

I'm a third-year BSIT student at Pampanga State University (Bacolor). I enjoy turning everyday problems into working software, especially apps that people at school or work actually use.

For our capstone, our team built a mobile app that monitors teacher punctuality, which we tested with real students, teachers and admins and successfully defended in November 2026. I've also built a grocery inventory-to-cashier system, a shop item sorter, a weather app, and the manufacturing module of a class-wide business system. I use AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude every day to work faster, and I always double-check what they give me.
